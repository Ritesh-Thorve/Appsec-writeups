# Hack The Box - Practical Progress

## Overview

This repository documents hands-on practice and progress on Hack The Box (HTB), covering machine exploitation, challenge solving, and applied penetration testing skills. It serves as a record of technical growth and practical proof of work in offensive security, built through direct engagement with realistic, vulnerable environments.

Write-ups here focus on methodology and technique rather than simply listing flags, reflecting a structured approach to enumeration, exploitation, and post-exploitation across a range of machine types and difficulty levels.

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to demonstrate:

- Practical, hands-on experience with real-world attack scenarios in a controlled environment.
- A consistent, methodical approach to reconnaissance, vulnerability identification, and exploitation.
- Familiarity with a broad range of vulnerability classes and attack surfaces, from web applications to network services and privilege escalation paths.
- Ongoing skill development through active participation on the platform.

## Areas Covered

- **Enumeration and Reconnaissance** - Service discovery, fingerprinting, and identifying attack surface on target machines.
- **Web Application Exploitation** - Common and advanced web vulnerabilities encountered on machines and challenges.
- **Privilege Escalation** - Linux and Windows privilege escalation techniques, including misconfiguration and kernel-level paths.
- **Challenges** - Standalone categories such as web, crypto, reversing, and forensics, tackled independently of machine boxes.

## Write-up Structure

Each write-up generally includes:

- **Target Type and Difficulty** - The category and difficulty rating of the machine or challenge.
- **Enumeration** - Steps taken to identify services, technologies, and potential entry points.
- **Exploitation** - The technique used to gain initial access or solve the challenge.
- **Privilege Escalation** - Where applicable, the path taken from initial foothold to full compromise.
- **Key Takeaways** - Lessons learned or techniques worth reusing in future engagements.

## Disclosure Note

In line with Hack The Box's policy, write-ups for active or recently retired machines are withheld or delayed as required, and only retired machines are documented in detail here.

## Platform

Ongoing practice is conducted on Hack The Box, spanning machines, challenges, and Pro Labs as part of continuous, hands-on security skill development.
